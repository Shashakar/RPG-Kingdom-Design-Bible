# Vertical-slice experience specification

**Status:** Source-recorded milestone scope from [VERTICAL_SLICE.md](https://github.com/Shashakar/RPG-Kingdom/blob/main/docs/VERTICAL_SLICE.md).

## What the first complete experience must prove

The player can understand a settlement's needs, enjoy the journey to address them, and return to a materially changed place.

| Sequence | Player action | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Observe ruins and meet residents | Clear needs and immediate motivation |
| Preparation | Gather wood, stone, food, and iron as authored | Legible resources and usable inventory |
| Recovery | Restore three to five buildings | Authored stage changes and settlement consequences |
| Adventure | Explore the region and enter one dungeon | Threats and rewards relevant to restoration |
| Climax | Defeat one dungeon boss | An observable progression consequence |
| Return | Revisit settlement | Permanent, comprehensible change |
| Continuity | Save and reload | Recovered world state remains intact |

## Explicitly out of scope

Online multiplayer, procedural content, freeform construction, full resident schedules, multiple villages or regions, complex crafting, a dynamic economy, and full class/character creation.

## Playtest prompts

Ask players to explain what the settlement needs, why they are entering the dungeon, and what changed on return. Observe where they hesitate rather than teaching them the intended route. Separate *can complete* from *understands and enjoys*.

## Exit criteria

The slice is not validated solely by automated tests. External players must understand restoration without developer explanation and recognize the settlement as something they helped recover.
