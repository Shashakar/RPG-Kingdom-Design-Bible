# Visual direction — decisions still needed

**Status:** Open design work, not approved art direction.

The existing asset strategy identifies reusable sources but does not itself establish a complete art bible. The following must be decided and documented before agents can consistently deliver the expected visual quality:

- Overall mood, palette, saturation, and contrast across settlement, wilds, and dungeon.
- Environmental material hierarchy and ground-cover transitions.
- Terrain tile expansion rules and authored landscape composition standards.
- Vegetation density, tree placement, scale, and silhouette language.
- Architectural cohesion and damaged/restored visual continuity.
- Camera-based readability, route framing, and tutorial-friendly navigation.
- Character, enemy, and equipment visual cohesion across third-party packs.
- Review evidence format and visual acceptance rubric.

Avoid inventing approvals. Attach annotated in-engine images and record trade-offs for each choice.
