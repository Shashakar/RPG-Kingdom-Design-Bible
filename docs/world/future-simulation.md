# Autonomous settlements and regional economies

**Status:** Exploratory future directions, **not committed roadmap scope**.

## A world that does not wait motionless

A village might eventually respond to a threat using local guards, seek outside help, receive aid from NPC adventurers, or suffer regional consequences if responses fail. The intended player experience is a world with agency and readable cause-and-effect—not constant mandatory maintenance.

A possible escalation story: **local threat → village response → success or worsening danger → request for aid → outside intervention or regional damage**. This is an illustrative future model, not a shipped system.

## Regional markets without universal access

Settlements may produce and need different goods, with local stock and prices influenced by geography, supply, danger, and trade connections. The design direction explicitly avoids a universal auction house that trivializes scarcity and distance.

Gathering should remain meaningful, but the player should not be forced to personally gather every material forever. Trade is intended to support restoration and adventure, not turn the game into a spreadsheet simulator.

## Multiple characters and persistent world

A future direction considers multiple characters participating in one persistent world, with clear separation of character-owned and world-owned progression. Account/character/world ownership details remain open.

## Boundaries

These directions do **not** turn Rekindle into an MMO, grand-strategy game, or mandatory city manager. Do not prematurely build village AI, a market simulator, regional calendar, or multi-character infrastructure for the vertical slice.
