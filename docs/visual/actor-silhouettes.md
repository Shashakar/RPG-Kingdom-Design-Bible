# Character and enemy silhouette references

**Status: Playtest art selection recorded; permanent actor visual standard open.**

The initial Polysplit Games Base Characters are a practical **playtest** choice. Do not interpret their proportions or finish as the permanent art direction.

## Required comparison sheet

| Role | Evidence to capture | Readability question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player | Neutral pose, equipped weapon, action pose | Is the player recognizable against terrain? |
| Melee hostile | Idle, approach, attack | Is melee intent obvious? |
| Ranged/caster hostile | Windup, cast, recovery | Can the player read the threat? |
| Boss | Player-scale comparison, attack silhouette | Does the boss read as distinct without UI? |
| Town resident | Idle and work gesture | Is the character identifiable as a person with a role? |
| Wisp/Spectre | State variants of one identity | Is partial restoration legible? |

## Approval requirements

Any candidate permanent actor family needs gameplay-camera screenshots beside actual Townsmith, Fantasy Adventure Environment, and MysticDungeon content, with animation and URP compatibility evidence.
