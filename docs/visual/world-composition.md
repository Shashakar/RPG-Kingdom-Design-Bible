# World composition reference sheet

**Status: Proposed creative evaluation criteria; palette and art direction undecided.**

## Reference targets

| Study | Desired evidence | Emulate | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Settlement street | Player-height forward and reverse views | Clear road hierarchy, believable entrances, differentiated building roles | Uniform structures and arbitrary prop scattering |
| Forest transition | Village-to-woodline-to-road sequence | Vegetation grouped by terrain, coherent path edges, visual depth | Abrupt material seams and repeated tree spacing |
| Dungeon approach | Far, medium, and near views | Legible destination, escalating mood, navigable route | Featureless passageway and hidden objectives |
| Restored location | Matched before/after framing | Same-place recognition and purposeful new activity | Complete visual replacement that erases identity |
| Tutorial space | Movement/camera path with landmarks | Discoverable route and enough clearance for teaching controls | Decoration that obstructs instructions |

## Existing project evidence

Use the [source-linked long-view screenshots](existing-game-evidence.md) as a starting point. Their existence does not mean the compositions are approved.

## Unresolved decisions for creative director

1. Overall stylization target: proportions, detail density, texture treatment.
2. Color and lighting palette: warmth, contrast, saturation, time of day.
3. Vegetation density and distribution rules.
4. Architecture and materials across damaged, recovering, and restored states.
5. Minimum acceptable quality bar demonstrated by annotated positive and negative examples.
