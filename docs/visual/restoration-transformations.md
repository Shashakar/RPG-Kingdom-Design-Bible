# Restoration transformation reference sheets

**Status: Mechanics are source-backed; visual design and final imagery remain proposed.**

## Building states

| Ruined | Stabilized | Restored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Damage and lost function are evident | Repair is visibly underway | Structure communicates regained purpose |
| Show the same location and recognizable silhouette | Retain landmarks and continuity | Make usable entrances and services legible |

**Needed imagery:** three screenshots of the *same building*, at the *same camera pose*, with the three authoritative Building Restoration states. Do not substitute three different buildings.

## Soul states

| Wisp | Spectre | Restored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragmentary identity | Partially recovered identity where authored | Coherent, identifiable person |
| House resident may skip Spectre | Blacksmith reaches Spectre after Forge | Blacksmith reaches Restored after Hammer hand-in |

**Needed imagery:** matched-character variants, same pose/scale where possible. The visual language must not imply that every soul requires Spectre.

## World consequences

**Needed imagery:** matched view of a blocked route and its unlocked state, plus evidence that the restored space is navigable and interactable.

## Review criteria

Continuity, player comprehension, visible causal progression, asset consistency, in-motion behavior, and correct state after Save/Continue.
