# Approved starting art direction

**Status: Approved creative direction — starting style family.** Human selection recorded October 19, 2026, following comparison of stylized, semi-realistic, and hybrid visual directions.

## Chosen direction: Stylized fantasy

The selected direction is **Stylized Fantasy (Option A)**. It prioritizes recognizable silhouettes, legible architecture and pathways, coherent asset families, and a welcoming fantasy aesthetic. It is a starting art direction, **not** a blanket approval of every element in the exploratory comparison image.

## Confirmed supporting preferences

- **World mood: balanced.** Settlements may feel welcoming, wilderness adventurous, and damaged areas threatening. The atmosphere should vary intentionally by region and state.
- **Environment detail: balanced.** Use natural vegetation grouping, distinct ground and structure materials, clear paths, and environmental storytelling without unnecessary clutter.
- **Asset compatibility:** Evaluate against the assets actually available in Rekindle-Claude; legacy RPG Kingdom art choices are candidates, not inherited approvals before inventing incompatible styles.
- **Restoration:** Changes should be readable at gameplay distance, preserve location identity, and produce visible emotional contrast.

## Restored settlement atmosphere — approved refinement

**Grounded, but visibly alive.** Restored settlements should retain believable architecture, materials, and evidence of their history. Restoration brings back inhabitants, activity, and purpose—not simply brighter colors or greater saturation. Favor signs of daily life such as active workshops, residents at work, tended gardens, occupied homes, market activity, and selective warm lighting when appropriate to the scene. Avoid exaggerated cheerfulness, excessive decorative clutter, and a theme-park palette. This is a qualitative art-direction decision, not approval of specific lighting values, prop counts, or NPC schedules.

## Directional qualities to pursue

1. Readable stylized forms and recognizable landmarks.
2. Cohesive materials with enough variation to distinguish road, stone, wood, soil, vegetation, and damage.
3. Natural composition rather than uniformly spaced props or bare geometry.
4. Character and enemy silhouettes that stand out at normal camera distance.
5. Clear world-state progression without requiring UI to explain every change.
6. Lighting and color that reinforce local narrative rather than enforcing one global mood.

> **Creative decision — October 19, 2026:** The current **Rekindle-Claude character visual style is not approved and requires a complete redesign**. Treat its current characters as implementation placeholders, not reference targets. The overall stylized-fantasy world direction does **not** approve the existing character proportions, faces, armor styling, silhouettes, or visual finish. Preserve working gameplay/animation infrastructure where practical, but assess new character art independently. The character concept boards are exploratory, not approved replacements.

## Explicitly not yet approved

- Specific color values, lighting presets, shader treatment, or texture-resolution rules.
- A particular architecture kit layout, foliage density number, or terrain material blend.
- Permanent character models, armor proportions, or boss designs.
- Exact HUD layout, frame art, iconography, or typography.
- Any individual scene in the generated comparison board as a production composition.

## Implementation baseline

**Rekindle-Claude**, branch `rekindle-vertical-slice`, is the starting implementation. The older RPG Kingdom repository is optional reference material and may be replaced or retained; its asset strategy and playtest art are **not** automatically binding on Rekindle-Claude. Review [Claude baseline evidence](rekindle-claude-baseline.md) before applying any legacy art selection.
