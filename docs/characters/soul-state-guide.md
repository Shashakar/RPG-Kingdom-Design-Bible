# Soul-state presentation guide

**Status:** Wisp/Spectre/Restored semantics are canonical; detailed visual treatments remain proposed.

## Wisp

A weakly anchored soul. The source specifies a **golden/orange** visual language and fragmented communication. The player must still receive usable guidance. A Wisp can be mysterious without making the objective incomprehensible.

## Spectre

Partially restored identity. More recognizable form and speech, but important anchors remain absent. A Spectre should communicate *progress without completion*. Limited services or interactions are possible when fictionally justified.

## Restored

A coherent person with a fuller role and social presence. Restoration should be visible in both the character and the surrounding world.

## Proposed differentiation tests

- Can players distinguish the three states in grayscale or without relying exclusively on hue?
- Is the character's degree of coherence communicated by silhouette, motion, speech, or material treatment?
- Does the transition align with the recovered anchor?
- Is the NPC's role clearer after restoration?
- Are transitions legible without an explanatory menu?

## Authored NPC worksheet

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Name / role | Who this person is |
| Current soul state | Wisp, Spectre, or Restored |
| Required anchors | Place, possession, relationship, duty, memory |
| Visible state differences | Model, motion, effects, behavior |
| Interaction and objective | What the player learns and does |
| World consequence | What becomes possible |
| Approval evidence | Creative director's decision |
