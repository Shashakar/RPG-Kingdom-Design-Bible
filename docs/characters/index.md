# Characters, souls, and creatures

**Status:** Soul-state design is source-recorded. Silhouette and production-art criteria are proposed.

## The human story behind restoration

A core premise is that people may exist in three recognizable presentation states:

| State | What it means | Source-recorded expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wisp** | Weakly anchored soul | Golden/orange language; fragments, whispers, impressions |
| **Spectre** | Partially reconstituted person | More recognizable presence and coherent speech, but incomplete identity |
| **Restored** | Identity recovered for the authored scope | Coherent body, memory, fuller interaction, resumed role |

Not every character must traverse all three states. The player should understand *why* a person changes: a home, possession, relationship, duty, or memory anchor has been recovered.

## A character's identity is more than an asset

For each important NPC, record:

1. **Role:** What did this person contribute to the community?
2. **Anchors:** Which place, possession, relationship, duty, or memory matters?
3. **Presentation by state:** How do Wisp, Spectre, and Restored appearances differ?
4. **Player understanding:** What clue or objective communicates the next step?
5. **Consequence:** What service, relationship, or world change follows restoration?

Avoid a universal restoration token or a generic glow effect as the only distinction.

## Visual readability rubric — proposed

- **Silhouette:** identify player, friendly NPC, hostile melee, hostile caster, and major boss at gameplay distance.
- **Role cues:** weapon, stance, motion, and effects reinforce behavior before combat becomes chaotic.
- **Animation:** movement, gathering, combat, and work gestures have clear intent and timing.
- **Scale:** bodies, weapons, doorways, and props share a consistent authored scale.
- **State transitions:** Wisp/Spectre/Restored states are distinguishable without relying only on color.
- **Cohesion:** mixed asset sources must feel like one game rather than adjacent sample packs.

## Player identity

The player should obey the same soul-restoration metaphysics as the people they help. Their exact origin remains undecided. The canonical foundation prefers exploring a hybrid restoration origin but explicitly does **not** canonize it.

## Asset boundaries

The current playtest character presentation is not a permanent art-style decision.
