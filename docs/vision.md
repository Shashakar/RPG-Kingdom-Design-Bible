# Vision and player promise

**Status:** Source-recorded product direction; detailed visual treatment remains open.

## The fantasy

> Restore a damaged fantasy world through third-person adventure, making places visibly safer, healthier, and more alive.

The player should be able to recognize a lasting difference in the world because they acted. The likely production title is **Rekindle**; RPG Kingdom is still the development codename.

## Six guiding ideas

1. **Restoration over conquest.** Combat removes obstacles; renewed life is the lasting reward.
2. **Adventure feeds civilization.** Exploration, gathering, and dungeons recover the people, materials, and knowledge needed to rebuild.
3. **Progress is visible.** Buildings, people, routes, services, and atmosphere change—not just numbers in a menu.
4. **Management is optional.** Communities should function without mandatory micromanagement.
5. **Cooperation is future-facing.** Multiplayer should support flexible roles without rigid party requirements, but is outside the current slice.
6. **Authored meaning before procedural scale.** Prove the experience with handcrafted content first.

## Emotional arc

| Beat | Player understanding | World evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Something valuable was lost | Ruin, absence, blocked routes |
| Understand | Recovery has specific causes | People, anchors, needs, clues |
| Venture | Adventure can change the situation | Threats, materials, recovered knowledge |
| Restore | The player can repair more than objects | New inhabitants and services |
| Confront | Regional danger has an identifiable source | Authored dungeon and boss |
| Return | The world remembers what happened | Clear, persistent before/after changes |

## Experience boundaries

This is **not primarily** a survival game, city builder, loot treadmill, political simulator, sandbox construction game, or MMO. Those genres may inform individual mechanics without taking over the central fantasy.

## Quality filter for proposals

Ask whether a feature:
- makes adventure enjoyable;
- makes adventure enable restoration;
- makes restoration legible and emotionally meaningful;
- or preserves the resulting world state reliably.

If none apply, it probably does not belong in the current vertical slice.

## Unresolved creative decisions

The final player origin, definitive art palette, permanent character art style, production branding, and detailed soundscape are **not finalized here**. They require deliberate design review, not inference from temporary production assets.
