# Creative direction and technical architecture

**Status:** Working collaboration policy based on the stated project decision; pending explicit sign-off in this repository.

- **Creative direction:** Human creative director has final approval of visual and gameplay design.
- **Design Bible:** Stores reviewed creative intent, references, decisions, and acceptance criteria.
- **Claude:** Owns technical architecture in the RPG Kingdom implementation repository, subject to its documented contracts and human review.
- **Codex/Supervisor:** Implements scoped tasks under repository policies and scene-authoring grants.
- **Conflict rule:** A technical constraint does not silently rewrite approved design. Surface the conflict with options and consequences for review.
- **No circular authority:** Do not duplicate or override the full technical architecture in this design repository. Link to the [canonical architecture](https://github.com/Shashakar/RPG-Kingdom/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Change lifecycle

Proposal → human design approval → scoped implementation → evidence-backed visual verification. A merged PR alone is not a design sign-off.
